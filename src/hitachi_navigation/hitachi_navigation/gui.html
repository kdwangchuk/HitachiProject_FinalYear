Manual drive

Manual drive publishes to /tb3_1/cmd_vel. Stop the autonomous line follower before arming these controls; this dashboard cannot pause it.

Hold a direction or use the arrow keys / W A S D. Releasing stops the command. Speeds are capped at 0.10 m/s and 0.25 rad/s.
